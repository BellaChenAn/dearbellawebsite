The Gift Story +
In Canada, the geese don't wait for the light to turn green — they are the light. Every day, with unshakeable confidence and zero apologies, the Canada Goose leads the way across the street, goslings in tow, while cars, cyclists, and entire schedules come to a respectful halt. The sign says 1 mph. The goose does not care. This is their country, and we are simply living in it.
